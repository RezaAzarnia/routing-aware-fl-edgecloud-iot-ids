**Statistics (corrected wording; see `STATISTICAL_INFERENCE_NOTE.md`).** n = 5 paired seeds (n = 3 for CICIoT2023 sensitivity and all scalability arms). Per paired contrast: mean (SD) of the paired differences; 95 % *parametric* interval mean ± t(0.975, n−1)·SD/√n (assumes approximately normal paired differences, which cannot be checked with n = 5); and a *non-parametric* exact sign-flip (randomization) test of the mean difference, whose smallest attainable two-sided p is 0.0625 for n = 5 (0.25 for n = 3). Consequently **the exact sign-flip test cannot reach p < 0.05 at n = 5, but the t-interval can exclude zero** (a one-sample t-test on the same differences then gives p < 0.05). The two are different methods with different assumptions and are not contradictory. Where an interval excludes zero (338 of 1,743 paired rows) this is reported as "interval excludes zero (unadjusted, exploratory)": about 1,700 comparisons were computed without multiplicity adjustment, the normality assumption is unverifiable, and the exact test does not support p < 0.05. **No superiority or significance claim is made from these intervals.**

## A. Verified requirements completed
1. **Attack robustness (comment 12, partial).** Static, non-adaptive attacks at 10/20/30 % exposure-controlled sign-flip (SF), norm-preserving sign-flip (BSF 20 %) and label flip (LF 20 %), MAD+surrogate pipeline vs FedAvg, both datasets, 5 seeds (Table T1, Fig. 1). FedAvg degrades with SF exposure (UNSW selected AUC 0.982 → 0.946 / 0.887 / 0.705 at 0/10/20/30 %; CICIoT2023 0.996 → 0.995 / 0.981 / 0.958) and collapses at the last round (AUC 0.50 at SF 30 % in all seeds). The MAD+surrogate pipeline stays at 0.980–0.984 (UNSW) and 0.996 (CICIoT2023) at every SF level; paired Δ(ref − FedAvg) at SF 30 % is +0.275 [+0.135, +0.415] UNSW (5/5 seeds higher), +0.038 [−0.014, +0.089] CICIoT2023 (5/5 higher). BSF 20 % and LF 20 % do **not** harm either pipeline (Δ within ±0.006): these attacks are weak in this setup, and MAD retains 79–80 % of BSF updates, i.e. the filter does not detect norm-preserving flips, they are simply harmless here. With no attack the pipeline gives no benefit (Δ +0.0009 / −0.0001) and MAD rejects 5–7 % of honest updates.

## C. Findings that change the scientific interpretation (mandatory manuscript consequences)
1. **Uniform weights in the central-DP pipeline.** The weight cap is applied only in the central-DP aggregation path. There, with ≤ 10 retained updates (cap a_max = 0.1 infeasible) CapNormalize returns exactly 1/|K_t|. Verified on 2,940 DP aggregation rounds from 98 runs: all rounds with ≤ 10 retained updates (2,750) are exactly uniform (max deviation 1.4e-16); the only non-uniform rounds are N = 100, k = 20 (cap feasible) and 10 abstaining rounds. A unit test of the implemented function with adversarial raw weights (n = 1…20) confirms uniformity for n ≤ 10. Consequences: **χrec and sample-size weighting do not influence the applied weights in the proposed DP pipeline**; χrec=0 reproduces `reference_gaussian` bit-for-bit whenever no abstention occurs. In the non-private reference pipeline weights are *not* capped: they are sample-size × χrec, heterogeneous in every round (largest single share up to 0.51), and there χrec is active (but empirically inert for detection: |ΔAUC| ≤ 0.0008).
2. **Surrogates dominate more than the text states.** Mean surrogate weight share in the proposed DP pipeline 0.30 (reference_gaussian 0.304/0.306 on CIC/UNSW; pooled over proposed-pipeline DP arms 0.296); surrogate-only rounds 78 of 2,760 (2.8 %), 5 per 150 for the reference. Under random selection the share is 0.41–0.43 and 17 % of rounds are surrogate-only; under uniform participation 0.58–0.63 and 38–41 %. Mixed rounds give zero-raw-weight surrogates weight 1/n (retained unchanged for V1/V2 equivalence); this is why χrec=0 only changes results through abstention.
3. **Gaussian-only vs combined contrasts (audited, `audits/AUDIT_ablation_*.csv`).** `abl_reference_gaussian_repro` vs `abl_privacy_clip_only` differ only in noise (same weights, same clipping, identical traces 5/5): a clean noise-only contrast (Table 5 "Added Gaussian": −0.0015 CIC, −0.0157 UNSW). `abl_privacy_clip_only` vs `abl_reference_repro` ("Clipping + cap") is a **combined** contrast: clipping (95–100 % of updates clipped) **and** replacement of heterogeneous weights by exactly uniform ones (and the DP aggregation path). "Full privacy pipeline" and Table 4 reference-vs-ε10 / E3-vs-E1 are likewise bundles. They cannot be attributed to "clipping" or "capping" individually.
4. **Diagnostic accounting.** ε ≈ 9.9993 (planned 10) in 30 attempted rounds; with abstention the release-based diagnostic ε falls to 9.86 (1 abstention) / 9.43 (4) while the conservative attempted-round figure stays 9.9993. Both are RDP estimates under an assumed Poisson-subsampling model, not certified differential privacy.
5. **Clean data show no benefit of the framework.** Without attack, reference ≈ FedAvg; the gain is conditional on contamination. Sign-flip robustness is shown only against non-adaptive attacks.
