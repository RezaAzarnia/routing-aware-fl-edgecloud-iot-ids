## 2. Novelty distinction (evidence-based)
* **No entry that could be read contradicts our gap statement**: none of the 14 read (full or partial full text) combines route/delivery-scored client selection, a packet-level update-delivery model, surrogate imputation of missing updates, a median/MAD filter and participation-fairness measurement, or reports a pre-registered negative guard result.
* **Closest studies read in full:** almeida2025modular (same dataset UNSW-NB15, benign worker failures; no delivery model/route scoring/imputation/fairness), latif2026multimodel (50 clients, cosine-distance filtering, latency; static client-to-edge assignment; MEDIUM closeness), mughal2024adaptive (static network-quality node selection), zhang2026adaptive (FL trains a routing predictor, all nodes participate), alanazi2026edgeguard (task scheduling, random client sampling).
* **Residual risk** (cannot exclude): abdallah2026routing, liu2026reliability, tang2026fedcst (metadata only); haripriya2025fault, chen2025hierarchical, bhardwaj2024routing (abstract only); shah2026trisafe (abstract: Byzantine + DP on IDS datasets — overlaps in kind with our robustness and privacy elements, not in mechanism). No systematic literature search was performed.
* **Verdict on novelty:** the contribution is distinguishable as a *controlled measurement study* (joint reliability/participation/stability trade-off, single-factor ablation, MAD behaviour versus genuine-update count, pre-registered negative guard result, hash-locked protocol). It is **not** a new estimator or privacy mechanism, and "no prior work does X" must remain worded as "within the sources we examined". The manuscript already uses that wording.
